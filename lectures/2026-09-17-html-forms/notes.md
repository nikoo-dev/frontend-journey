# Lecture 4 — HTML Forms (2026-09-17)

Covered how forms collect user input: the `<form>` container, `<input>` and its
`type` values (text, email, password, number, date, time, color, file), and why
pairing `label` + `for`/`id` matters over just placing text near an input.

Went through key attributes — `placeholder`, `name`, `value`, `required`,
`readonly`, `disabled`, `min`/`max`, `minlength`/`maxlength` — plus radio buttons
(shared `name` = single choice), checkboxes (multiple choice), `textarea`,
`select`/`option` dropdowns, and grouping fields with `fieldset`/`legend`.

Noted that HTML's built-in validation (e.g. `required`, `type="email"`) is
useful but not a substitute for backend validation.
